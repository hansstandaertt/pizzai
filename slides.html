Artificial Intelligence

AI is not new

Artificial Intelligence has existed for decades. The recent boom is the result of multiple technological breakthroughs converging together.

1950s

First AI concepts and machine reasoning.

1980s

Expert systems and rule-based automation.

2010s

Deep learning and neural networks mature.

2022+

ChatGPT makes AI accessible to everyone.

Why now?

What made AI explode in recent years?

Transformers

New model architecture capable of understanding language context at massive scale.

Scale & GPUs

Huge datasets and cloud computing power enabled much larger models.

Usability

Chat interfaces made AI accessible to non-technical users.

AI became mainstream the moment it became usable and accessible for everyone — not just researchers.
Large Language Models

What is an LLM?

A Large Language Model predicts the next most likely token based on context.

Good analogy

Think of it as autocomplete on steroids.

Important nuance

LLMs do not “know” facts like a database.

MCP

What are MCP servers?

MCP allows AI systems to securely interact with enterprise tools and systems.

Without MCP

The model only knows its training data.

With MCP

The model can interact with Confluence, Jira and Bitbucket.

An LLM without MCP is like a very smart employee without an access badge.
